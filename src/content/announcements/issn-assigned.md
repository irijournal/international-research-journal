International Research Insights: Multidisciplinary e-Journal has been assigned the International Standard Serial Number **ISSN (Online) 2839-7134** by the ISSN National Centre for India.

The ISSN now appears in the header of every page of this website, on every article page and in the PDF of every article, including all five articles of [Volume 1, Issue 1](/current).

Authors citing work published in the journal may include the ISSN in their references. An ISSN identifies a serial publication; it is not an approval or a rating of the journal or its contents. The journal's indexing position is set out on the [indexing and preservation](/indexing-and-preservation) page.
