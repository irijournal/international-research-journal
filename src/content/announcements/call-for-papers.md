The editorial board of International Research Insights: Multidisciplinary e-Journal (ISSN (Online) 2839-7134) invites original manuscripts across all seven research sections.

**Last date of submission: 30 November 2026.**

## Sections open

All seven research sections: Law and Constitutional Studies; Health and Nursing Sciences; Hindi Language and Literature; History, Heritage and Culture; Education and Pedagogy; Society, Economy and Public Policy; and Science, Technology and Environment. Interdisciplinary work is welcome.

## Article types

Research articles, review articles, case comments, short communications and book reviews, in English or Hindi.

## What happens after you submit

Every submission receives a written editorial response within seven working days. Manuscripts that pass screening go to double anonymous peer review by two reviewers, and the reports are shared with the author with the decision.

## Processing charges

Payable only after acceptance: ₹1,500 for a single author, ₹2,000 for two or three authors (plus ₹500 for each author beyond three), ₹5,000 for papers in science, technology and the health sciences (USD 100 and USD 150 for authors from outside India). There is no charge at submission or if a paper is not accepted. See [processing charges](/processing-charges).

## How to submit

Read the [submission guidelines](/submission-guidelines), complete the [author declaration](/author-declaration), and submit through the **[online submission form](https://forms.gle/3C8Ksvb4vjYAdRey5)**. If you cannot use the form, email the files to submissions@internationalresearchinsights.com.
