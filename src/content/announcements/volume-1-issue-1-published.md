The founding issue of International Research Insights: Multidisciplinary e-Journal, Volume 1, Issue 1 (July to September 2026), is now published online.

## In this issue

1. [A Quinquennial Empirical Analysis of Case Pendency in Indian High Courts and the Imperative for Expedited Adjudication](/articles/case-pendency-indian-high-courts), Dr. Ravi Kumar
2. [Beyond the Ballot: M. N. Roy's Theory of Organised Democracy and the Problem of Popular Sovereignty in India](/articles/mn-roy-organised-democracy-popular-sovereignty), Puru Sharma
3. [A Study to Assess the Level of Stress among B.Sc. Nursing Students in a Selected Nursing Institute of Guwahati, Assam](/articles/stress-among-bsc-nursing-students-guwahati), Jaiprakash Tyagi
4. [A Study to Assess Knowledge, Skills and Practice Regarding Patient Safety among B.Sc. Nursing Students of Regional College of Nursing, Guwahati, Assam](/articles/patient-safety-knowledge-skills-practice-nursing-students), Dr. Ramavatar Singh Tyagi
5. [Neuropsychiatric Disorders: A Comprehensive Overview](/articles/neuropsychiatric-disorders-comprehensive-overview), Akhileshar Prasad Mishra

Each article has its own page with the abstract, full text and a PDF. The complete table of contents is on the [current issue](/current) page.
