## The short version

Processing charges are payable **only after a paper is accepted** for publication. There is no charge at submission, no charge during peer review, and no charge if a paper is not accepted.

## Charges per accepted paper

| Category | Authors from India | Authors from outside India |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single author | ₹1,500 | USD 100 |
| Two or three authors | ₹2,000 | USD 100 |
| Four or more authors | ₹2,000 + ₹500 for each author beyond three | USD 100 |
| Papers in science, technology and the health sciences | ₹5,000 | USD 150 |

Science, technology and health sciences papers are those in the [Science, Technology and Environment](/sections/science-technology-and-environment) and [Health and Nursing Sciences](/sections/health-and-nursing-sciences) sections, including nursing, pharmacy and medical research.

## What the charge covers

- Copy editing and typesetting in the journal's format.
- Publication of the article page and PDF, with volume, issue, month, year and page numbers.
- Permanent archiving and open access to readers under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
- An e-certificate of publication for every author of the paper.

## How and when to pay

The editorial office sends the acceptance letter first. The invoice follows with the payment details, and the paper is published once payment is received. Payments are accepted only into the journal's official account named in the invoice, and a receipt is issued for every payment.

## What the journal never charges for

The journal does not charge for submission, does not sell faster review, and does not offer guaranteed acceptance. Editorial decisions are made on the manuscript alone and are never influenced by payment. The journal writes to authors only from addresses ending in @internationalresearchinsights.com.
