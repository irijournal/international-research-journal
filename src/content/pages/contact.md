The editorial office answers every message within five working days. If you have not heard back in that time, write again and mark the subject line `REMINDER`, because the first message has gone astray somewhere and the fault is ours.

## Who to write to

| If you are writing about | Use | Subject line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new manuscript | The submissions address | `SUBMISSION | Section | Type | Surname | Short title` |
| A manuscript already with us | The submissions address | `MANUSCRIPT [number] | your query` |
| Joining the reviewer panel | The general address | `REVIEWER PANEL | Section | Surname` |
| Scope, fit or format questions | The general address | `QUERY | your question in five words` |
| A complaint or an appeal | The Editor in Chief | `APPEAL [manuscript number]` or `COMPLAINT` |
| Permissions and copyright | The Editor in Chief | `COPYRIGHT | article title` |
| Indexing, partnership or institutional matters | The general address | `INSTITUTIONAL | organisation name` |

Manuscripts are accepted by email only. They are not accepted through this page, through social media or through messaging applications, because a manuscript needs an attachment, a declaration and a record.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement of a manuscript within two working days, with a manuscript number.
- A reply to general correspondence within five working days.
- A substantive response to a complaint within four weeks, acknowledged within five working days.
- No message left unanswered, including those the journal must decline.
